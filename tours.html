Indulge in a Private Wine Tour

Explore the world-class wineries across Australia in the ultimate comfort of a Lux VIP vehicle. Our private wine tours allow you to set your own pace and itinerary, ensuring a truly personalized experience.

Sit back and enjoy the scenic drive while our chauffeur takes care of the navigation. Taste the finest wines, enjoy gourmet lunches, and discover hidden gems without the worry of driving.

Tour Highlights:

  • Custom Itineraries: Visit your favorite wineries or let us recommend the best spots.
  • Swan Valley & Margaret River: Tours available for both premier regions.
  • Group Travel: Our vehicles are perfect for groups of any size, whether friends or family.
  • Door-to-Door Service: We pick you up and drop you off at your doorstep.
  • Venues & Lunch: We can also book venues and lunch packages for you.

Suggested Itinerary (Standard 5hr Tour):

  • 11:00 AM: Pick up from location
  • 11:30 AM - 1:00 PM: First Stop
  • 1:15 PM - 2:45 PM: Second Stop
  • 3:00 PM - 4:30 PM: Third Stop
  • 5:00 PM: Drop off at location
